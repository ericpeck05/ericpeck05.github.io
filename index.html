§ 03
About

I am a fundamental investor before I am anything else. I hold for years rather than quarters, I want to understand a business well enough to describe it to someone who has never heard of it, and I would rather read the filing than the headline about the filing.

The code came second. Every tool here started as something tedious I was doing by hand, and the useful part was never the program, it was learning exactly where my own process was sloppy once I had to write it down as steps.

I am working toward a career in wealth management, where the interesting problem is not picking securities in a vacuum but fitting a portfolio to a real person's life, their taxes, their timeline, and what they actually intend to leave behind.
