Data center site finder

Grid trading bot

Python, Coinbase Advanced Trade API

SOL grid bot on GitHub Binance version (no dependencies)

A strategy that ladders buy and sell orders across a price range and works the grid as price moves through it. I built it to see how execution costs, fees, and slippage chew through a strategy that looks clean in a backtest. That gap is the actual lesson.

Crypto signal research bot

Python, SQLite, scikit-learn

Collects perpetual futures funding rates, on-chain metrics and social sentiment on a schedule, turns each into a z-score so they are comparable, and blends them into one directional signal per asset. It trains and backtests on that signal, and routes orders only through a risk layer with position caps, stops, a daily loss limit and a drawdown kill switch. Most of the work was the risk layer, which was the lesson.
